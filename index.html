How it works

Press Listen and start reading. The prompter matches what you say against the script and keeps your next line on the amber marker. Pause, ad-lib, or skip a sentence and it picks you back up.

Put stage directions in brackets, like [pause] or [look at camera 2]. They show in blue and are never listened for.

Keys: Space listen on/off · ↑ ↓ nudge a word · tap any word to jump there · Esc back to script.

Privacy
0 / 0

Send your voice online?

With this on, your browser's maker receives the audio from your microphone while you're listening, to turn it into text. Their privacy policy applies, not this app's.

It switches back to On this device the next time you open Cuefollow.

What Cuefollow stores and sends

Your voice

  • On this device (the default): speech is recognised by a model running inside this browser. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Browser's online speech (only if you turn it on): your browser sends audio to Google (Chrome), Microsoft (Edge) or Apple (Safari). It turns itself off every time you reopen the app.

Stored in this browser

  • Your script and display settings.
  • The speech model, about 40 MB per language, so it works offline after the first download.
  • A copy of the app's files for offline use.

Sent over the network

  • Only requests for this app's own files, from this site. A security policy blocks the page from contacting any other server. There are no analytics, ads, trackers or accounts.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub sees your IP address when the app's files load. It never sees your script or your voice.
