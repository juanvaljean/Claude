How it works

Press Listen and start reading. The prompter matches what you say against the script and keeps your next line on the amber marker. Pause, ad-lib, or skip a sentence and it picks you back up.

Put stage directions in brackets, like [pause] or [look at camera 2]. They show in blue and are never listened for.

Keys: Space listen on/off · ↑ ↓ nudge a word · tap any word to jump there · Esc back to script.
